A little about me

I moved from Italy to London in 2018. Settling into a new country, a new school and a new language taught me to adapt quickly.

That is a big part of why I enjoy computing. Most of programming is getting comfortable with something unfamiliar, one small step at a time. Before university I completed my GCSEs at Chace Community School in Enfield, and now I build small projects alongside my degree to practise what I learn.

Italian is my mother tongue. My English is C1 in listening, reading, writing, spoken production and spoken interaction, and I also speak Albanian.
